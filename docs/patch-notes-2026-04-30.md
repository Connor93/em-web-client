# Patch Notes — April 30, 2026

## New Features

### Aura Effects — Expanded Library

- Auras gained seven new visual effects to mix and match: rolling-shadow body distortion, radial pulses, spiral vortex warping, motion-blur streaks, spectral chromatic ghosting, soft dreamy bleed, and animated lightning bolts arcing around the character.
- All seven work on **player auras**; the lightning overlay also renders correctly on **weapon auras** and in the encyclopedia weapon-aura preview.
- Six new sample player auras showcase the additions: **Lightning Lord** (crackling bolts), **Wraith** (heavy distortion + chromatic ghosting), **Maelstrom** (shockwave halo), **Vortex Mage** (spiral + lightning combo), **Hyperdrive** (zoom-blur streaks), and **Dreamwalker** (soft pastel hue).
