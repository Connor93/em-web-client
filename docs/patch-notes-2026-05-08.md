# Patch Notes — May 8, 2026

## New Features

### Weddings

- Talking to a Lawyer NPC now opens a dialog where you can register a fiancé or file for divorce.
- Talking to a Priest NPC starts the wedding ceremony — enter your fiancé's name to send a marriage request, then your partner gets a prompt to accept and the priest performs the ceremony.
- Both partners receive an "I do!" prompt at the right moment during the ceremony.

## Improvements

### Combat Feedback

- Attacking while overweight now shows a warning toast instead of failing silently, so it's clear why your swings aren't connecting. The warning is rate-limited so it won't flood the screen if you hold or spam attack.
